The processor

PMP-1550

Zero-Gate Passive Matrix Processor. A glass panel carrying a model’s weights as printed optical paths.

Weight cell
Non-resonant passive attenuator. Weight = light intensity, so path-length and phase errors do not move it.
Material
Optical polymer on glass, printed with UV lithography; nanoimprint at volume.
Power in bank
0 W. Power goes to the laser, modulators, detectors and converters.
Optical scope
MLP layers, about 81% of multiplies per token. Idle mixture-of-experts weights draw nothing.

How we fill the facility.

OI:LOOP sells tokens, not chips. Each model we serve becomes a set of printed panels in racks in our hall. Space is a cost line, not a limit, and the panels themselves draw no power.

Questions.

Short answers about the company, the technology and access.

What is OI:LOOP?

OI:LOOP is an early-stage company that prints the weights of large language models into passive optical processors and sells low-cost AI inference tokens from its own facility. It does not sell chips.

How does a passive optical processor run an AI model?

The input is written onto 1550 nm light. The light passes a glass panel where each printed path transmits a fixed share of light, and that share is the weight. Detectors sum the products. Every multiplication of a matrix happens in one pass of light, and electronics handle activations and attention.

How much energy can it save?

In OI:LOOP's core-level design model, the passive bank uses 3.3–6.3 femtojoules per multiply, 30–60 times less than a current flagship GPU at about 200 fJ. This is a design model, not yet a measurement.

Why does the weight bank use no power?

The weights are fixed optical paths, not memory cells. There are no heaters, transistors or clocks in the bank, so it draws 0 W. Power goes to the laser, modulators, detectors and converters.

Which models will OI:LOOP run?

First a half-billion-parameter language model end to end, then a ~100B-class mixture-of-experts pilot, then 700B-class models. One printed panel set carries one model version.

What stage is OI:LOOP at?

Architecture, material choice, energy model and accuracy simulation are complete. Nothing has been fabricated yet. The next milestone is printing and measuring an 8×8 weight bank, then a 64×64 tile.

Why 1550 nm?

1550 nm is the wavelength of fibre-optic networks, so lasers, fibres, modulators and detectors are mature, low-cost telecom parts.
